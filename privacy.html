Yesman 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日

我们不收集任何数据

Yesman（iOS / iPadOS / macOS）不收集、不存储、不出售任何个人信息。App 内没有账号系统，没有广告、统计或崩溃上报组件。

数据如何流动

屏幕画面、声音、键盘鼠标输入和剪贴板内容只在你自己的设备之间传输：经由你自己的 Tailscale 私有网络（WireGuard 端到端加密）或你的局域网直连，不经过我们运营的任何服务器。

保存在你设备上的内容

你添加的主机地址与名称、App 设置保存在设备本地；配对密钥保存在系统钥匙串中。删除 App 即可清除。

权限

相机：仅用于扫描 Mac 上显示的配对二维码，图像不保存、不上传。本地网络：用于连接你的 Mac。Mac 端需要“屏幕录制”和“辅助功能”权限，用于采集画面和执行你在手机上的操作，内容只发送给你配对过的设备。

联系我们

如有疑问，请通过 App Store 页面上的开发者联系方式与我们联系。

Yesman Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

We collect no data

Yesman (iOS, iPadOS and macOS) does not collect, store or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

How data moves

Screen images, audio, keyboard and pointer input, and clipboard text travel only between your own devices — over your own Tailscale network (end-to-end encrypted with WireGuard) or your local network. Nothing passes through servers operated by us.

What stays on your device

Saved host names and addresses and app settings are stored locally; pairing keys are stored in the system Keychain. Deleting the app removes them.

Permissions

Camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code shown on your Mac; images are never saved or uploaded. Local Network is used to reach your Mac. On the Mac, Screen Recording and Accessibility are used to capture the screen and perform your input; content is sent only to devices you have paired.

Contact

For questions, use the developer contact on the App Store page.
